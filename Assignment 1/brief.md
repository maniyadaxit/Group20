# CampusEats System Brief

## What

CampusEats is a campus food ordering system that connects students and other campus users with food vendors. Users can browse available menus, select food items, place orders, and check the status of their orders. Food vendors can receive orders, manage their menus, and update the status of each order. The system aims to make food ordering on campus more organized and convenient while reducing waiting time.

## Who

The main users of CampusEats are:

* **Students:** Browse food items, place orders, and track order status.
* **Campus Staff:** Order food from available campus vendors.
* **Food Vendors:** Manage menus, receive orders, and update order status.
* **Administrators:** Manage users, vendors, and overall system information.
